# Shared paper artwork

`paper-fill.png` was generated with the built-in image_gen tool for the Mafia and
Spyfall UI. It is a bundled offline material texture, consumed by the shared
Godot nine-slice paper styles and browser styles. Original vector paper sources
remain available. Unselected drafts and design previews stay in `.test_data`.

## Production paper fill prompt

```text
A seamless production UI paper fill texture, square 1024x1024, FULL BLEED: every pixel is opaque paper, absolutely no outside background or alpha. Warm pale ivory / cream archival paper. Delicate very fine natural fibres, subtle low-contrast wrinkles, faint old floral filigree impressed into the surface, tiny understated speckles. Overall very light, calm, even ivory material for clear black typography in a vintage Spyfall dossier UI. The whole image is the interior surface of the paper. Do NOT depict a sheet or a border: no torn edges, no deckled outline, no burned edges, no brown frame, no holes, no corner folds, no dark corners, no silhouette, no shadows, no rolled parchment. No writing, no symbols, no stamp, no objects. A simple premium material texture with soft irregular detail at almost-uniform light brightness; looks like the clean paper interiors in an elegant illustrated spy dossier mockup.
```
