# Battleship artwork

The supplied `ChatGPT Image Sep 30, 2026, 11_26_16 PM.png` was used as the
reference. The original on the Desktop was left unchanged. These three raster
assets were made with the built-in ImageGen tool and copied into this module.
`title.png` and `ship.png` retain transparent alpha. No remote assets are loaded.

Saved project assets:

- `Games/battleship/art/ship-bridge.png`: full-screen bridge and ocean scenery.
- `Games/battleship/art/title.png`: glossy title and wave cutout.
- `Games/battleship/art/ship.png`: gray toy naval ship cutout.

The prompts below are the final prompts used. Board frames, cannon and markers
are reproducible module-owned SVG geometry from `generate_vectors.py`.
Fleet monitors on the host and controllers share five SVG profiles generated by
that script: carrier, battleship, cruiser, submarine and destroyer. Their common
scale follows ship lengths 5/4/3/3/2; the two size-three ships have distinct shapes.
Sound source credits and the offline Foley build are in `../audio/SOURCES.md`.

## ship-bridge.png

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: full-screen background artwork for an offline Battleship game.
Input image: the supplied Battleship screenshot is a visual reference and an edit target for removing UI.
Primary request: preserve the illustrated toy-like ship bridge, bright blue tropical ocean, lighthouse on the left island, distant gray warship on the right, sunny blue sky and fluffy clouds, gray metal bridge supports with red signal lights, vivid green radar at the far left, brass binoculars at the far right, paper charts, compass, ropes and deck details at bottom. Match the supplied art's saturated colors, glossy materials, lighting and playful high quality 3D illustration as closely as possible.
Change: REMOVE the entire BATTLESHIP title, BOTH central blue and red game-board frames and their grids, ships, labels and markers, all fleet labels/counters, and the two bottom PLAY / RESTART buttons. Fill their former areas naturally with open ocean, bridge railing and uncluttered metal deck. Leave the central 65% quiet and available for real interactive boards and the title. Side radar/binoculars sit near the outside edges. No game UI, no writing, no lettering or numbers anywhere.
Composition: wide 16:9, straight frontal camera like reference. Full bleed, no border, no watermark. Preserve the supplied scenery style; no characters.
```

## ship.png

Asset type: transparent game sprite, a single ship viewed from above.

A long streamlined silver hull, pale metallic deck trim, dark blue-gray side, three small gun turrets, compact central bridge, short mast, portholes, a few highlights and soft contained contact shadow. Look just like a premium 3D toy game piece.

Fully isolated on genuine transparent alpha. Fill the image with ship, leave just a small transparent margin. No water, no wake, no grid, no base pedestal, no UI, no lettering, no character face, no watermark. Ship must remain easy to recognize at small board-cell size.
