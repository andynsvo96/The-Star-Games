Monopoly Classic

Buy properties or send declined purchases to auction. Complete a color group to build evenly. Sell buildings for half price before mortgaging or trading developed streets. Mortgage repayment costs the principal plus 10%. Free Parking pays no cash.

Doubles grant another roll; three consecutive doubles send you to Jail. Leave Jail with doubles, $50, or a Jail Free card. A third failed attempt requires $50 before moving.

Points mean total wealth: cash, properties, and buildings, adjusted for mortgages and outstanding debts. Standard play ends with one solvent player. Timed play selects the wealthiest player; ties use cash, then dice. Exactly one player wins and everyone else loses.

Any AI action, including timeout autoplay, makes the whole game practice. It will never update saved wins, losses, or profile points. With the turn timer off, disconnected players must reconnect. Your host can save and resume unfinished games.
